Sky Strip. Floating track, three checkpoints, one loop. Jet hard. Open the air brakes to land where you want, or with the jet on, to fly.

Right thumb: GAS, BRAKE and JET. Left thumb: steer and AIRBRAKE. Tap TILT to steer by turning the phone, where the browser allows it.

W S

A D
Steer. In the air with air brakes held: roll
A/D + tap S
Drift. In a drift A/D change the angle, no input holds it; steer out until straight to end it

Space
